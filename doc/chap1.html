1 Introduction

The FusionSystems package tests whether saturated fusion systems with given properties could exist on a finite p-group S, without constructing them. Its main functions are:

  • AllProtoEssentials (2.2-1) finds the subgroups of S that could be essential in a saturated fusion system on S, up to S-conjugacy, and IsProtoEssential (3.1-1) tests a single subgroup (Chapters 2 and 3);

  • PossiblySupportsReducedFusionSystems (4.1-1) tests whether S could support a reduced fusion system (Chapter 4);

  • FindReducedSequences (5.1-1) finds the automizer sequences that could lead to reduced fusion systems (Chapter 5).

The tests check necessary conditions only: a subgroup found to be proto-essential, or a group that passes the tests for reduced fusion systems, need not lead to an actual saturated fusion system.

To follow the progress of a longer computation, set the info level of InfoFusion to 1 or higher.

gap> S := SmallGroup(2^7, 931);;
gap> Length(AllProtoEssentials(S));
5
gap> PossiblySupportsReducedFusionSystems(S);
true
